### Business problem:
---
The Superstore generates sales across different product categories, customer segments, geographical locations and shipping modes. However, without a structured analysis, management may find it difficult to understand which products and customer groups contribute most to sales, which locations generate the highest revenue, and how sales performance changes over time.
The business therefore needs a sales performance analysis to identify its strongest revenue sources, uncover sales patterns and highlight areas that may require management attention.

### Business objective:
---
To analyse the Superstore's historical sales data using Microsoft Excel, identify sales trends and revenue contributions across products, customer segments and geographical locations, and provide actionable recommendations to support sales planning, product decisions and customer engagement.

### Dataset Description and Source:
---
The source of the Dataset analyzed in this project is Kaggle. The data contains sales from a Retail  Superstore from the period of 2015 - 2018. It contains 9,800 rows and 18 columns. The field names are Row ID, Order ID, Order Date, Ship Date, Customer ID, Customer Name, Segment, Country, City, State, Postal code, Region, Product ID, Category, Sub-Category, Product name and Sales. The dataset was clean but the datatype for each field was changed to the right datatype, For Example Order Date and Ship Date datatype was changed to Date.

### Tool Used: 
---
  - Microsoft Excel(Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, visualization) 
  
### Business Questions:
---
The following business questions were analyzed.
- How has sales performance changed over time?
- Which product subcategories generate the highest sales?
- Which regions contributes most to total sales?
- Which customer segments contribute most to sales?
- Which customers contribute the highest sales value?

### Key findings
---
- Monthly Sales Trend: 
Sales were strongest in month September, November and December and weakest in month Januray and Febuary. This suggests a possible seasonal pattern in customer purchasing behaviour.
- Annual Sales Trend: 
Sales increased from 2015 to 2018, indicating growth in the business's revenue performance. The highest sales were recorded in 2018, while 2015 recorded the lowest.
- Sales by Region: 
West Region generated the highest sales, contributing N710,220, while South Region recorded the lowest sales.
- Sales by Product Subcategory: 
Phone Subcategory generated the highest sales, while Fasteners Subcategory recorded the lowest. 
- Sales by Customer Segment: 
The Consumer segment generated the highest sales, contributing N1,148,061, compared with other customer segments.
- Top 10 Customers by Sales: 
The top 10 customers contributed N153,810 in sales, with Sean Miller recording the highest individual sales value.

Note: High sales do not necessarily mean high profit. A product may generate substantial revenue but still be unprofitable. The current dataset cannot establish that.

### Recommendations
---
1. Management can use the monthly pattern to guide inventory planning, sales campaigns and resource allocation. They can also investigate recurring low-sales periods for possible opportunities to stimulate demand.
2. Sales grew during this period of analysis, management should investigate and identify the products and customer segments contributing to the growth.
3. Management should Examine the products and customer segments driving sales in high-performing regions. For lower-performing regions, they should investigate product demand, customer reach and potential market opportunities before allocating.
4.Prioritise the availability and promotion of high-revenue product groups. Investigate low-revenue subcategories to understand whether the issue relates to limited demand, product selection or market visibility.
5. Management can strengthen engagement with the segment contributing the most revenue while identifying opportunities to grow sales among segments with lower contributions.
6. Management can develop customer retention and relationship-management initiatives for high-value customers. They can also investigate to know if the business depends heavily on a small group of customers.

### Limitation
1. No profit information: The dataset does not contain Profit or Cost, so profitability and profit margin cannot be assessed.
2. No quantity information: The analysis focuses on sales value rather than the number of units sold.
3. No discount information: The impact of discounts on sales cannot be evaluated.
4. No Shipping Time: Although Ship Mode and Ship Date is available, Shipping time, delivery time and shipping efficiency cannot be measured.
5. Historical data: Findings describe the period covered by the dataset and should not automatically be treated as current market conditions.
6. No customer demographics: The analysis cannot explain customer preferences by age, gender or income.

### Conclusion
The Superstore Sales Performance Analysis examined historical sales data to understand revenue trends, product performance, geographical contributions and customer purchasing patterns. Using Microsoft Excel, The analysis identified the major contributors to sales and areas requiring further business attention. The findings provide management with insights to support sales planning, customer engagement and product-market decisions.
